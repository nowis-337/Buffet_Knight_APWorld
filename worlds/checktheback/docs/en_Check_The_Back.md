## What is the goal of Silver Daze when randomized?

Get your paycheck by flipping two switches and opening the safe in the office. This requires defeating three bosses.

## What items and locations can get shuffled?

Locations in which items can be found:
- Dust piles
- Defeated enemies
- Lockers
- Trash Cans
- Refridgerators
- Bosses

Based on your settings, some locations may only drop filler and are not included in the pool.


Items that can be shuffled:
- Skills that make bosses possible to beat
- Items that increase your power
- Party members to aid you
- Items that let you unlock lockers and pick up dust piles

## What does another world's item look like in Silver Daze?

Items picked up do not change appearance

## When the player receives an item, what happens?

Items are sent to the storage shelf and need to be retrieved periodically.
